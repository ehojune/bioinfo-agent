# Publication routes

Checked 2026-10-20. This is a fit/workload assessment, not an acceptance-rate
estimate. One month is a preparation/submission target, not a peer-review promise.
Author composition and institutional arrangements remain private decisions.

| Route | Fit and remaining work | Cost boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **SoftwareX: Original Software Publication** | Closest format fit for reusable research software, architecture and demonstrated use. Prepare a public release, reproducible examples and the required template; current guide: 4,000 words, at most 6 figures. | Fully OA; a **Hybrid-only** agreement does not cover it. Obtain a separate quote/funding decision. |
| **Bioinformatics Advances: Application Note** | Closest specialist readership. Demonstrate usefulness, quality and accessible examples; 4 pages and 3 figures/tables combined. | OUP; an Elsevier/Wiley agreement does not establish coverage. |
| **Wiley Software: Practice and Experience: Research Article** | Plausible Hybrid route for original implementation work on OS/runtime/virtualization and reusable components. Needs generalizable engineering results and comparison; validation-only papers are outside scope. | Optional OA or traditional publication. Agreement journal list, article type, corresponding-author eligibility and remaining quota still need confirmation. |
| **Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine** | Conditional Elsevier candidate for biomedical methods/software systems. Needs a convincing biomedical application and comparison. Current author-guide/article-type details remain unverified in this audit. | Confirm current Hybrid eligibility; do not confuse it with the fully OA companion **Update**. |
| **JOSS** | Deferred: current screening requires more than six months of active public development and demonstrated research use. This repository was created 2026-07-28, so its history cannot meet that gate by 2026-11-20. | No submission or publication fees. |
| **bioRxiv research preprint** | Free dissemination after screening. Include research results, tool outputs or public-data reanalysis; a software announcement or stand-alone protocol is insufficient. | No submission charge; screening is not journal peer review. |

## Shared preparation

Prioritize 2–3 reproducible public cases and a matched control/recovery experiment
over adding pipelines. Record raw evidence, task denominators, wall time, peak RSS
and reuse under the same conditions. [The proposed comparison](related-work.md#evaluation-contract-before-claiming-an-advantage)
has not been performed.

Useful comparators are [Colony](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformaticsadvances/article/6/1/vbaf304/8343633)
for accessible containerized analysis and [Reproducibility as a service](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/spe.3202)
for provenance, containers and recovery. These are related engineering systems,
not evidence of this project's novelty or expected acceptance.

Choose authors by actual contribution and responsibility. An APC agreement alone
does not justify authorship. Confirm ownership and the filing/publication sequence
before a new public preprint; see [submission and filing](submission-filing.md).
Bioinformatics Advances has a restrictive manuscript-AI policy: use these
repository records as checked research inputs, and have researchers author the
submission under the journal's current rules.
